# Event equipment protection

## Transit damage
Accidental impact damage to rented lighting equipment during transport is covered up to EUR 7,200. Damage caused by unattended outdoor storage is excluded.

## Theft
Theft of rented sound equipment is covered only when a police report is provided within 48 hours. Loss by misplacement is excluded.

## Outdoor displays
Weather damage to rented projection screens is covered only when the organizer obtained a weather permit before the event.

## Mechanical malfunction
Internal electrical failure of rented generators is covered up to EUR 4,800. Damage from using an incompatible fuel is excluded.
